The Story Behind the Small Things

It all started with a dusty box of broken jewelry from my husband's grandmother—total vintage goldmine vibes. I saw magic in those forgotten sparkles and started turning them into new stories.

What began as a fun side project has become my passion: creating art that's sustainable, whimsical, and seriously unique. Every piece is handmade right here in Columbus, Ohio, using upcycled costume jewelry, seashells, buttons, and vintage treasures rescued from thrift stores and estate sales.

No two pieces are ever alike. Each one carries a bit of history, a lot of love, and maybe a little weirdness—and I wouldn't have it any other way.

— Marika
